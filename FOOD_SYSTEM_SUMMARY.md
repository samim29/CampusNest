# 🍽️ Food System Quick Reference

## Two Food Systems Overview

### 1. **Campus-Wide Food (Mess & Food Page)**
**Purpose**: Discovery, variety, social dining
```
Access: Main Nav → "Mess & Food"
Sections: Mess | Restaurants | Online Orders
Users: All students (day scholars + PG residents)
```

### 2. **PG-Specific Food (PG Detail Page)**
**Purpose**: Accommodation meal planning, monthly budgeting
```
Access: PG Details → "Menu & Food" Tab
Sections: Meal Plans | Weekly Menu | Dining Hours | Dietary Options
Users: PG residents and prospective tenants
```

## Key Differences

| Feature | Campus Food | PG Food |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Scope** | University-wide | Accommodation-specific |
| **Duration** | Per meal/day | Monthly plans |
| **Price** | ₹120-250/meal | ₹2,000-4,500/month |
| **Purpose** | Variety & social | Planning & budgeting |
| **Users** | All students | PG residents only |
